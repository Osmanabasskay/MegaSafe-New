How do I enable two-factor authentication? expand_more
To enable 2FA, navigate to Security Settings > Authentication. You can choose between SMS codes or an authenticator app. We highly recommend using an authenticator app like Google Authenticator or Authy for maximum protection.

What happens if I lose my recovery phrase? expand_more
Your recovery phrase is the only way to access your vault if you lose your password. MegaSafe does not store these phrases for your security. If you lose it, we cannot recover your data. We recommend storing it in a physical fireproof safe or a safety deposit box.
Can I share my vault with family members? expand_more
Yes, our Family Plan allows you to share specific folders with up to 5 family members. Each member maintains their own private vault while having access to shared resources.
Is my data encrypted locally or on your servers? expand_more
MegaSafe uses zero-knowledge architecture. This means your data is encrypted locally on your device before it ever reaches our servers. We never have access to your unencrypted files or your master password.
